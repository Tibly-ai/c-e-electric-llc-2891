About C-E ELECTRIC, LLC

Our Expertise

At C-E ELECTRIC, LLC, we take pride in our experience in the electrical contracting industry. With over 30 years of combined experience, we handle projects that range from high-end luxury custom homes and complex underground utility upgrades to medical facilities. That same crew handles residential and commercial electrical services, so one company can cover the whole scope of a property's electrical needs.

Our licensed, experienced electricians deliver electrical solutions that are reliable and affordable. Call us and we will walk the job with you before anyone quotes a number.
